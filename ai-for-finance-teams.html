The half day

What the half day looks like

We run from 9:00 to 13:00 at your office, with a break from 11:00 to 11:15. All of it happens on your own files and your own systems.

01

9:00 to 11:00
Claude set up properly

Claude is the AI assistant the day is built on. We start with the privacy settings, then set up Projects (workspaces in Claude that keep your files and instructions together) linked to your own folders. I connect Claude to your accounting system, your spreadsheets and your Word reports, and set up work that runs on its own. Your team also shows me how they work now, so we build around that.

02

11:15 to 12:00
The tasks that eat your time

We pick the tasks that eat your team's time and automate them, on your real files, not a demo spreadsheet.

03

12:00 to 12:45
Your accounting system's own AI

Your accounting system has AI features of its own. We go through what to switch on, what to review before you rely on it, and what is still in beta, which means the supplier is still testing it.

04

12:45 to 13:00
An agent that runs every week

We build an agent together, which is a set of instructions Claude runs on its own. It runs every week without anyone starting it, and a person on your team checks what it produces.

What they walk away with

  • Claude set up to work in their own files
  • Routines for the tasks we picked
  • 1 agent running by itself
  • A clear view of what AI can do for them, including the AI inside their accounting system
  • Confidence using it, rather than nervousness

The systems

The systems I build on

Each of these has AI features of its own. They are partly in beta and change every month, so on the day I tell you what works, what needs a person to review it, and what is marketing.

Xero

JAX, Xero's assistant, matches bank transactions on Grow plans and up, reads receipts and bills, and answers questions.

QuickBooks

QuickBooks Accounting AI categorises bank transactions and flags likely errors; VAT AI checks figures before you file.

Sage

Sage Copilot, in Sage Accounting, drafts overdue payment reminders, flags likely mistakes and spots pay that looks unusual.

Zoho Books

Zia answers questions, drafts invoices and quotes from prompts and flags late payers; bank categorisation is in early access.

FreeAgent

FreeAgent's Smart Capture reads receipts and bills, extracts the details and matches them to bank transactions.

Sage Intacct

Sage Intacct agents track month-end close tasks, flag errors and outliers, and match supplier bills to purchase orders.

Business Central

Copilot in Business Central proposes extra bank reconciliation matches and suggests general ledger accounts for unmatched lines; in preview.

NetSuite

NetSuite Bill Capture, an add-on, reads supplier invoices, fills in the bill and matches it to purchase orders.
